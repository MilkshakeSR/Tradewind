Tradewind 1.1.0

Navigation fixes, far fewer data requests, free heatmap and top movers, back navigation

Added

  • Back navigation: a back button named after where you came from returns you to the same list, scroll position, sort, filters and toggles. ⌘[ and ⌘] go back and forward, a two-finger swipe right goes back, and Esc closes a stock opened from search. Opening a peer goes back to the previous stock.
  • Optional split view (Settings → General) that keeps the list visible beside a stock.
  • Free plan setup: Twelve Data, Massive and Alpha Vantage join EODHD and Finnhub. Tradewind sends each request to the provider with the most allowance left and moves on automatically when one runs out.
  • Settings → Data: a comparison of the free plans, step-by-step sign-up with links, a Test button for each key, the cheapest paid upgrade, a usage meter per provider with a breakdown by feature, and editable limits.
  • Sector heatmap on free plans: a treemap grouped by sector and sized by market cap, with Today, 1 Week, 1 Month and YTD, a colour-blind option, and a saved view when live data isn't available.
  • Top movers on free plans: gainers, losers, most active and unusual volume, with a minimum price and size filter, sparklines, a watchlist button and a note of where the data came from.
  • A What's New screen, shown once after updating.

Changed

  • Tradewind now opens from saved prices and fetches only what's on screen and out of date. Launch makes far fewer requests.
  • Prices refresh every 60 seconds while a market is open and on screen, and not at all once a market has closed and the last price is from after the close. Refreshing pauses in the background and during sleep, and slows on battery.
  • Each request has a priority, so what you're looking at always comes first and background extras pause first when the allowance runs low.
  • The ⌘1 … ⌘9 sections are listed in the View menu, and each section keeps its place when you switch away and back. The last section is remembered between launches.
  • Paper Trading positions and portfolio holdings now use a simple grid layout.

Fixed

  • Clicking the sidebar now switches sections.
  • "Your data plan has reached its limit for today" no longer appears on launch. If a limit really is reached, Tradewind switches provider, then shows saved prices with a small note in the toolbar.
  • Invalid keys, per-minute limits, daily limits and network errors each get their own correct message.
  • The sector heatmap and top movers no longer come up empty without a paid plan.
  • Fixed a crash when leaving the Portfolios section.
