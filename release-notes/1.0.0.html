Tradewind 1.0.0

First release

Added

  • Market overview: ASX, NYSE and Nasdaq status with countdowns in Sydney time (local time on hover, daylight saving handled), index cards with sparklines (ETF stand-ins when a data plan has no index values), AUD/USD, gold, oil and Bitcoin, top movers, a sector heatmap you can click into, market news and this week's calendar.
  • Search (⌘K) across ASX and US stocks and ETFs with exchange badges, live prices and recent searches.
  • Stock pages with tabs (⌘T, ⌘W): price header with pre-market and after-hours prices, an interactive chart (1D to Max; line, area, candlestick and OHLC; volume; SMA, EMA, Bollinger Bands, VWAP, RSI, MACD, Stochastic, ATR and On-Balance Volume; crosshair, drag to measure, scroll or pinch to zoom; compare mode; dividend, earnings, split and trade markers; saved price and trend lines), 30 key statistics with plain-English explanations, and Overview, Financials, Earnings, Dividends, Analysts, News, Ownership, Peers and My notes tabs.
  • Insights: an overall signal from −100 to +100 built from trend and momentum, valuation, quality and growth, and market view, with adjustable weights and presets, key support and resistance levels, "what would change this signal", and a five-year backtest against buy and hold.
  • AI Analyst (optional, your Claude API key): ask about any stock, plus an optional daily portfolio summary. Answers are balanced, say when data is missing and end with the General Advice Warning.
  • Watchlists with drag-to-reorder, icons and colours, customisable sortable columns, a card view and live price flashes.
  • Portfolios in any base currency: buys, sells, cash dividends, DRPs, splits, fees and transfers with the exchange rate on the trade date; CSV import for CommSec, Stake, SelfWealth, Pearler and CMC Invest plus a reusable column mapper with duplicate detection; holdings, performance against a benchmark, time-weighted and money-weighted returns, currency effect, dividend income, allocation charts, upcoming events, concentration and rebalancing insights.
  • Australian tax helper (an estimate): realised gains by financial year with the 50% CGT discount, FIFO or specific lots, franking credits and a CSV export for your accountant.
  • Paper trading with pretend money: market, limit and stop orders, simulated brokerage, AUD/USD conversion, performance against the ASX 200 and S&P 500, a trade journal and trading stats, all marked PRACTICE.
  • Alerts for prices, daily moves, moving-average crossings, RSI, 52-week highs and lows, volume spikes, signal changes, earnings, ex-dividend dates and news, delivered as macOS notifications with a View button, with history and quiet hours.
  • Screener over ASX and US stocks with saved screens.
  • Calendar of earnings, ex-dividend and payment dates, rate decisions and market holidays.
  • Learn: a glossary of every term in the app, linked from the ⓘ buttons.
  • Menu bar ticker with a mini watchlist.
  • Data from EODHD and Finnhub with your own keys (stored in the Keychain), Frankfurter exchange rates, ASIC short interest, a rate-limited request queue, a disk cache and offline mode; realistic demo data when you have no keys.
  • Settings for General, Data (with a usage meter), Insights, Alerts, Portfolio, AI, Appearance (dark by default, colour-blind mode), Privacy, Backup (JSON export and weekly automatic backups) and Updates.
